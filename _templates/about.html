Our story

Why we started this.

Between us, we've spent the best part of two decades each building things: products from zero to one, teams, and change programmes across startups, scale-ups, consultancies and large organisations. We come at it from different angles, but we've landed on the same conviction: in any major technology shift, the technology is rarely the hard part. The human side is. AI is the biggest shift either of us will work through, and too often it gets handed to the IT team or the risk team, as if it's just a tooling problem. It isn't.

We've both watched the same thing play out more times than we'd like. A consultancy arrives with a deck. Six months later there's a strategy document, a handful of pilots that never scaled, and a team no more capable than when they started. The invoices get paid, but the capability never turns up, because the deck was treated as the deliverable. Slides and a big final playback are just moments. The actual work happens in between, with the people who have to live with whatever gets left behind.

Accelerator X was built to do the opposite. The senior people who scope your engagement stay on it and stay accountable for delivering it, not a partner who wins the deal and hands you off to a junior team once it's signed. Where it helps, we bring in specialists matched to what you actually need. The commitment is simple: real capability, owned by you, with everything pointed at the value you actually get.
