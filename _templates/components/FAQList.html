Questions

The questions clients ask first.

  • What happens after we apply?

    We read every application personally. If it looks like a fit, we'll reply within a week with a few follow-up questions or a proposed next step — usually a short call to confirm scope and timeline. If it's not right, we'll say so clearly and, where we can, point you somewhere useful.

  • How much does it actually cost?

    The "from" price is the floor. Final cost depends on company size, number of people involved, travel logistics, and how much pre-work we do together. We scope it transparently before any commitment — you'll know the number before you sign anything.

  • Do we need to have started on AI already?

    No. Most of our clients have tried things internally — with mixed results. Some haven't touched AI at all. The work we do is calibrated to where you actually are, not where we assume you should be.

  • How much time does this take from our team?

    The working sessions are scheduled in advance — typically half a day per week over the cycle. We run them efficiently. The bigger ask is that a senior decision-maker stays connected throughout. The value compounds when leadership is genuinely in it.

  • What if we don't get 10× ROI?

    We've never had a cycle where we didn't find it — but we build the measurement criteria into week 1 so there's no argument about what counts. If we're heading off-track at the midpoint, we recalibrate. We don't wait until week 8 to flag a problem.

  • Do you work with regulated industries?

    Yes — we've worked with financial services, healthcare, and professional services firms with real compliance requirements. We know how to build AI capability without creating regulatory exposure. It changes the scoping conversation, not the ability to do the work.
