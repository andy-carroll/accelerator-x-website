Honest about fit

Is this for you?

✓ Right fit if

  • You're a decision-maker who can commit the team's time
  • You want something shipped — not a strategy deck
  • You're ready to have honest conversations about what's blocking you
  • You have an area of the business you want to move fast

✕ Probably not for you if

  • You need sign-off from six people before committing any budget
  • You're looking for someone to write a report and leave
  • You want AI to do everything without changing how the team works
  • You're in a regulated environment that can't move at sprint pace
