Our story

Why we started this.

Between us, we'd spent two decades watching the same pattern repeat. A board gets excited about AI. A consultancy arrives with a deck. Six months later the organisation has a strategy document, a handful of pilots that never scaled, and a team that's more confused than when they started. The consultancy's invoices are paid. The capability isn't there.

We knew what the problem was. The work wasn't being done in the room — it was being done in a presentation. The people who needed to change their behaviour weren't being changed. The systems being built weren't owned by anyone who'd maintain them. And the partners who sold the engagement had left by the time the real work began.

Accelerator X was built to do the opposite. Founder-led, embedded, with a bias to shipped systems and a guaranteed handover. We work with a small number of clients at any time — because what we do only works if we're fully in it.
