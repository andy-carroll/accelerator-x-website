01 · Brand

A practitioner brand.

Credibility and precision. Practical intelligence. Energy without volume. The visual system is editorial in restraint, modern in palette, and engineered for reliability across every surface AX work travels through.

04 · Surfaces

Two families: cool and warm.

A page picks one family and stays there. Cool for marketing and conversion surfaces — white-based, energetic. Warm for editorial reading — paper-toned, considered. Never mix on the same page.

Cool family

White is the default.

Home, overview pages, forms, applications, conversion flows. Energetic, contemporary, business-forward. Navy text on white, pink for emphasis, cyan for action.

--bg-1 (#FFFFFF) · --bg-2 (#F8FAFC) · --bg-3 (#F1F5F9)
Warm family — new in v2

Paper, for reading surfaces.

Articles, case studies, methodology longform, the About page. The warm tone reads as considered, editorial, calm. Navy text on paper, pink accents still earn the eye. Use when the visitor is meant to settle in.

--bg-paper (#FAF8F3) · --bg-paper-deep (#F1EDE2)
Dark family

Navy carries weight.

Hero sections, section dividers, footer bars, the title slides of every deck. White text, pink accents earn maximum punch on this surface. Used for the brand's loudest moments — restrained but present.

--bg-dark (#1B2A4A) · --bg-darker (#131D30)

11 · Motion

Motion principles.

Motion exists to inform, not decorate. Quiet by default. Fast for interactions, slower for reveals. Always honour prefers-reduced-motion.

Durations

  • --dur-fast · 150ms — hover, focus state changes
  • --dur-base · 200ms — button press, small state change
  • --dur-slow · 300ms — modal entry, page-level state
  • --dur-page · 600ms — scroll reveal, large block entry
  • --dur-reveal · 800ms — generous reveal, hero moments

Easings

  • --ease-out · default — interactions feel native
  • --ease-in-out · transitions between states
  • --ease-spring · subtle bounce, used very sparingly

Standard patterns

  • Hover lift — translateY −2px + card-hover shadow, 200ms ease-out
  • Fade-up on scroll — opacity 0→1 + translateY 20→0, 600ms ease-out, triggered at 80% viewport
  • Link underline expand — width 0→100%, 200ms ease-out, left-anchored
  • Pink X bullet on appear — scale 0→1, 300ms ease-spring, used on list items entering view

17 · Voice

How we write.

Direct. Credible. Challenging. Human. Practitioner voice, not consultant voice. Short sentences. Active. Real numbers.

Principles

  • Direct. Say it once, clearly. No preamble.
  • Credible. Practitioner voice. Avoid jargon.
  • Challenging. Make the audience feel something — seen, slightly uncomfortable, then clear.
  • Human. Warm but not soft. Not corporate. Not agency-casual.

Banned phrases

leverage · ecosystem · unlock potential · transformative journey · synergies · best-in-class · world-class · thought leader · step-change · value-add · north star (non-literally) · low-hanging fruit

Before / after

Don't
We leverage our deep expertise to unlock transformative AI potential across your enterprise ecosystem.
Buzzword soup. Says nothing.
Do
Eight weeks. Clear scope. At least 10× the value of what you invest.
Specific. Measurable. No hedging.
Don't
Differentiators
A label, not a thought.
Do
What makes us different.
Headers tell, don't label.
