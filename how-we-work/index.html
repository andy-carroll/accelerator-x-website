How we work

A clear path from overwhelmed to operational.

Most consultancies optimise for billable days. We optimise for the day you no longer need us. Here's exactly how we do it.

Four principles

How we show up in every engagement.

01

Founder-led, always.

Toby and Andy in every session. No associates, no juniors running ghost-projects. The people you meet are the people who deliver.

02

We do all three.

People, process, and product. We build the systems, change the behaviour, architect the strategy — and stay until it ships. The seams are where the value lives.

03

The best outcome is you outgrow us.

We train your people, install systems that work without us, and step out when you're ready. No lock-in. No dependency. We're trying to get you self-sufficient.

04

Week one has to earn its keep.

If we don't add value in the first session, you don't pay for it. We're confident enough in what we do to back it with a guarantee.

Our approach

Four moves. End to end.

Every engagement runs through the same four practices — from first diagnostic to final handover. Each is designed to leave your team stronger than we found it.

01

Diagnose before you act.

Every engagement starts with a structured 2-session DOTS diagnostic. We map your AI surface, identify the opportunities worth pursuing, and align your leadership team before we touch anything. No assumed solutions. No pre-packaged answers.

02

Work embedded, not remote.

We work in the room with your team. Founder-led, session by session. We don't send a junior to take notes and report back — we show up, get our hands dirty, and do the work alongside you.

03

Ship, don't strategise.

We bias toward working systems. Every engagement ends with something that runs in production — not just something that impresses in a board presentation. Strategy serves shipping, not the other way around.

04

Transfer capability by design.

Everything we build, your team understands. Every session leaves skills behind. We measure success by how quickly you no longer need us — and we build the handover in from day one.
